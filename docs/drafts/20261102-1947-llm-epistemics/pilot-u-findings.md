## Bounded decision

**Retain source-linked notes as the provisional default. Shelve graph-by-default
and further broad graph development. Do not implement/deploy M1 or run another pilot.**
The available paired quality discordances are balanced, while this qualified subset
has a consistent reported-token disadvantage for graph assignment. No concrete narrow
graph capability benefit is demonstrated here. This is an engineering allocation
judgment, not proof that graphs are intrinsically inferior or cannot help elsewhere.

Preserve existing CQ4 task/dependency/provenance/authorization safeguards, scoped
bindings/history, admission ordering, CAS, idempotency, rollback and non-Task progress
restrictions. Freezing development does not authorize their removal, a migration,
application work, deployment or push. M1 stays documentation-complete, integrated and
archived, unimplemented and behaviorally unvalidated. The local r7 skill is preserved
but inert/uninstalled; no new useful nonoverlapping skill candidate was found by the
focused delegated review. Current remote draft persistence remains separately
qualified in the research interpretation, not silently overwritten.

Any future work requires a new concrete capability/decision/independent-assessment/
exit-criterion proposal and authorization. This entry is not that authorization.
